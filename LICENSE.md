## 3. ROYALTY

### 3.1 The rate

Royalty is calculated **per Title**, against that Title's **lifetime** Gross Revenue, in US dollars.
The bands are **marginal**: each rate applies only to the portion of lifetime Gross Revenue that
falls inside its band, in the same way income tax brackets work. Crossing a threshold never
re-prices the money you earned below it.

| Lifetime Gross Revenue for a Title | Royalty on that portion |
|---|---|
| The first **$1,000,000** | **Nothing.** |
| Above $1,000,000, up to $10,000,000 | **5%** |
| Above $10,000,000, up to $50,000,000 | **1%** |
| Above $50,000,000 | **1%**, subject to the cap in 3.2 |

### 3.2 The cap

Once a Title's lifetime Gross Revenue passes **$50,000,000**, the total royalty payable for that
Title in any single calendar quarter is capped at **$200,000**. Anything the calculation would have
produced above that cap in a quarter is not owed, and does not carry forward into a later quarter.

Below $50,000,000 there is no cap.

### 3.3 A worked example

A Title reaches $12,000,000 in lifetime Gross Revenue.

| Band | Amount in band | Rate | Royalty |
|---|---|---|---|
| First $1,000,000 | $1,000,000 | — | $0 |
| $1,000,000 → $10,000,000 | $9,000,000 | 5% | $450,000 |
| $10,000,000 → $12,000,000 | $2,000,000 | 1% | $20,000 |
| **Total** | | | **$470,000** |

The same Title later reaches $60,000,000. The $50,000,000 → $60,000,000 portion accrues at 1%
($100,000), and in any quarter where the amount coming due would exceed $200,000, only $200,000 is
payable for that quarter.

**You owe nothing, and need report nothing, until a Title's lifetime Gross Revenue passes
$1,000,000.** Until that happens there is no paperwork of any kind.

Once a Title crosses that threshold:

- Notify the Licensor within **45 days** of the end of the calendar quarter in which it crossed.
- For that quarter and each quarter after, provide a short statement of the Title's Gross Revenue
  for the quarter and its lifetime total, and pay the royalty due, within **45 days** of quarter end.
- Keep ordinary business records sufficient to support those statements for **three years**. The

### 3.5 What the royalty does not apply to

The royalty is on shipping commercial products, not on working. It does not apply to revenue that is
not attributable to a Title, and it does not apply at all to:

- Free games and projects that generate no revenue.
- Anything covered by Section 5 — tutorials, courses, books, videos, and the templates, assets and

  selling them content to build with, never triggers a royalty.
- Contract or work-for-hire development fees you are paid to build something, where you are not the
  one selling the resulting product. Whoever ships and sells it owes the royalty on it, if anyone
